Privacy Policy for NovaShift: Universe

Last updated: October 14, 2026

NovaShift Games provides NovaShift: Universe. This policy describes the current Android version of the game.

Data and local storage

The game does not require an account. Game progress, settings and diagnostic logs are stored locally on your device. Screenshots you choose to capture are also stored on your device. We do not transmit this information to our servers or share it with third parties.

You can remove the game's local data by clearing the app's data in your device settings or uninstalling the app.

Advertising and purchases

The current version does not display ads or offer working in-app purchases. If these features are added in a future version, we will update this policy and the Google Play data safety information before releasing that version.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this page when the game's features or data practices change. The date above will show the latest revision.

Contact

For privacy questions, email novashift.game@gmail.com.
